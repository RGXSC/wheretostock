    Decision 1 — target sell-through

    A 100% target means producing exactly the forecast. A lower target means producing more than the forecast. You choose once, and it cannot be changed.

    80% sell-through

    Decision 2 — where does the stock go at product launch?

    Decide which part of the production goes to the boutiques before launch; the rest stays in the central warehouse, where you can send it week by week — it takes one week to arrive, and no more than 30 pieces can leave in any one week. The boutique share is split equally between Paris and Tokyo.

    50% in the boutiques

    Both decisions are final once the game starts.
