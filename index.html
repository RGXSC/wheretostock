Briefing & setup

The numbers, before you decide

  • You are launching 5 bags in 2 stores, supplied by 1 central warehouse, over 25 weeks.
  • The forecast is 2 pieces per week, per store, per bag → 100 pieces per bag, 500 in total.
  • Every bag sells for 1 000 € and costs 200 € to produce.
  • You pay the 200 € for everything you produce — whether it sells or not.
  • A fixed cost of 225 000 € (45% of forecast sales) is charged at the end.
  • Missed sales are lost for good. There are no back-orders.
  • Your profit is revealed at week 25. During the game you only see units and revenue.

Decision 1 — target sell-through

How much of what you produce do you expect to sell? A 100% target means producing exactly the forecast. A lower target means producing more than the forecast, to protect against under-estimating. You choose once, and it cannot be changed.

Choose a target to see what gets produced.

Decision 2 — where does the stock start?

Part of the production goes to the shops before opening; the rest stays in the central warehouse, where you can send it week by week — but it takes one week to arrive. The shop share is always split equally between Store A and Store B.

Choose a split to see where the stock goes.

Both decisions are final once the game starts.
