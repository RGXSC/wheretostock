    Decision 1 — target sell-through

    A 100% target means producing exactly the forecast. A lower target means producing more than the forecast. You choose once, and it cannot be changed.

    Decision 2 — where does the stock go at product launch?

    Decide which part of the production goes to the shops before launch; the rest stays in the central warehouse, where you can send it week by week — it takes one week to arrive. The shop share is split equally between Store A and Store B.

    50% in store
