Fast forward — one season, five cities

The same five bags and the same thirteen weeks as the game, squeezed into a single shot, and played across five boutiques instead of two.

One rule is different here. The warehouse resupplies a boutique the moment it runs out. In reality that is one to three days, against a bag that sells once a week or once a fortnight — close enough to instant that this page treats it as instant. Everything central can therefore reach whichever city turns out to want it.

Two decisions, both taken before the season and never revisited: what target sell-through to produce against, and what share of that production to place in the boutiques at launch. The launch push follows each city's own forecast. Whatever is not pushed stays central.

The forecast

Two pieces per city per bag per week, over thirteen weeks — 650 in all, spread the way the collection and the network are actually weighted.
